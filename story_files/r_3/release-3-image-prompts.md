GENERATION NOTES

Character consistency, Ethan is lean, slim, soft jawed, brown hair, early twenties, always slightly tired looking. Jess is brunette, athletic, sharp featured, early twenties. Riley is androgynous, buzzed sides, flannel aesthetic.

No faces shown directly in most scenes. Use over shoulder, partial, silhouette, or environmental shots to maintain player projection.

Color palette, deep purple for the LED strips, warm amber for the kitchen and restaurant, cold blue for the phone and monitor screens, and black. These four colors carry the entire visual identity.

Aspect ratios, all scene images 16:9. UI elements at their native ratios. Phone screen close ups can be 9:16 vertical.

Avoid stock photo aesthetics, overly clean environments, bright daylight scenes. Everything in Release 3 happens indoors or at night. The world is small and getting smaller.
