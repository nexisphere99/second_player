GENERATION NOTES

Style consistency, all backgrounds should share a cohesive color palette leaning toward muted blues, purples, and warm ambers. The apartment scenes should feel small and slightly suffocating. Any Marcus related imagery should feel polished and aspirational by contrast.

Resolution, generate at minimum 1920x1080 for backgrounds, 1024x1024 for character art, variable for UI elements.

Color grading, the apartment scenes should have a slight desaturated quality, like the color has been slowly draining. Screen based images, Marcus content, social media, should be vivid and saturated, representing the digital world that's more alive than Ethan's physical one.
